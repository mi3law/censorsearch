    What the results mean
    • Likely banned (a filled sign): the title and author match a listing, or the title matches one that gives no author, or the listing covers the whole series, or the ISBN matches.
    • Worth a look (a dashed sign): a similar title, or the same title by another author. Check it is the same item before you act.
    • Titles with no match aren't shown. That does not mean they are permitted: the banned list is the list that counts.
