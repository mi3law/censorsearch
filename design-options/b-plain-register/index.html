About this page

“Updated as of” is the date written in the sheet by the people who keep the list. “Fetched” is the time this page last read the sheet.

The Google Sheet is the source of truth; this page only reads it. Nothing is stored: the list is fetched when the page opens and kept only in this browser tab, and what you type is searched in your browser and sent nowhere. Source code
